Flagship experiment — news wording A/B (real brain)

What this experiment is. The same Federal Reserve policy statement is published to the market at t=150 in two wordings: variant A (green) neutral — “policy remains data-dependent; risks roughly balanced”; variant B (red) alarming — “inflation risk elevated; tightening possible”. Everything else — population, mechanism, brain, seeds — is identical. The question: does the wording alone move the market? Read the chart as: if green stays above red after the intervention, framing matters — direction and ranking only, never effect sizes.

Where the persona data comes from

  • Panel structure (883 agents; class mix and AUM/flow weights) — derived from published market-share aggregates (Fed Z.1 fund-flow categories, SCF/ICI ownership surveys), coarse by construction.
  • Behavioural parameters (attention, news sensitivity, disposition, herding, risk aversion) — literature priors from behavioural-finance and brokerage-activity studies, deliberately uncalibrated. Every parameter carries a field-level evidence record (runtime/provenance.py).
  • Identity cards — archetype templates plus deterministic dispersion; no field is copied from a real person.
  • Human grounding today: 0%. The attributes a real person would fill (redemption history, statement-check frequency, fee sensitivity…) stay unset and are listed per agent as unknown — never invented. The closed-value investor survey and its evidence-patch pipeline (runtime/fields.py) exist to close exactly this gap, with consent, in the calibration split.

Red lines (every number on every tab obeys them)

  • Direction and ranking only — no effect sizes, no point predictions.
  • Model output is not evidence; rationale text is audit material.
  • Instability across models/prompts/seeds is a deliverable, reported as such.
  • Personas are calibrated priors, not real people; field-level provenance on every card, 0% human-grounded today.

Reproducibility contract

  • Five-tuple manifest: population × intervention × mechanism × model × seed; same tuple → bit-identical rerun.
  • Every Intent carries provider / model / prompt_version / prompt_sha8 — the hash of the prompt actually sent.
  • Manifests carry code_hash over the runtime that produced the run.
